## Public dataset policy

The public CSV files are intentionally minimized:

- personal/team names are omitted;
- copied page-body snippets are omitted;
- no login-only or access-controlled content is collected;
- source URLs and observation metadata are retained for traceability;
- URL-status labels describe only the observed state of a URL at a specific time.

If you reuse the dataset, check the terms and rights of the original source sites independently.
